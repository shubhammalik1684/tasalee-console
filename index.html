By design

How much of a score the record can carry on its own

Weighted by category, per asset class. The rest needs a site visit, a counter, or a document the seller has to produce.

Eleven of the thirteen capping findings can be tested automatically, which is the number that matters more than the coverage percentage. A file can be cheap to screen and still be caught by the one thing that sinks it.

Standing rules for anything that leaves this console

State the coverage beside the score

A seventy on eighty percent coverage and a seventy on forty percent coverage are different claims. Both go on the page.

Name the source and the date

Every finding carries the source it came from, its evidence tier and when it was last refreshed. An assertion by a seller is tier six and is labelled as one.

Never publish the provisional geometry

Pilot footprints go no further than this console. A client is told the pocket, and told that the plot boundary is unverified.

The agent does not score alone

Below three hand scored files it keeps framework weights and says so. Above that it reports its leave one out error next to every number it produces.

Disclose what a source cannot prove

A jamabandi is a record of rights, not a title guarantee. A RERA registration is not a construction warranty. Both are written into the source map.

No client sees this vocabulary

Knockout, tier, divergence, viewstate and coverage are internal words. The client side of the same fact is written in plain language.

Gurugram resale stock is three different things

Revenue estate land

Wazirabad, Nathupur, Sikanderpur Ghosi, Chakkarpur, Kanhai, Badshahpur and the Sohna belt. Khasra and khewat are live and the jamabandi is authoritative. This is the only branch where a coordinate genuinely gives you a khasra, once the cadastral sheet has been rubber sheeted onto real coordinates.

HSVP sectors and licensed colonies

DLF Phases 1 to 5, Sushant Lok, South City, the numbered sectors. Once land is acquired and relaid the khasra stops being the property's identity. Identity is sector, block and plot, and the records sit with HSVP and DTCP rather than the patwari. The DTCP licence schedule lists the khasras the colony was carved from, which is the only bridge back to branch A.

Apartments and condominiums

Camellias, Magnolias and every tower like them. There is no parcel at unit level at all. The land is held as an undivided share and the unit is a floor space right, so identity is project, tower and unit, proved by the conveyance deed and the RERA registration.

Most specific identity wins

A single pin can sit inside a village, a colony carved out of that village, and a tower inside that colony. Specificity runs C, then B, then A. The superseded layers are still reported, because the revenue estate underneath is where the licence schedule bridges back to a khasra.

What is automated and what is not

Portals are discovered and monitored automatically. Gates that exist to stop machines, captchas above all, are cleared by a person. The artefact that carries legal weight is the certified copy ordered through the official channel, so nothing here substitutes a scrape for a certified record. The automation exists for speed, for surveillance, and for telling an analyst exactly where to look.

Look at the parcel before you trust the record

Paste a plus code, a coordinate, or a Google Maps link. Every imagery source that covers Gurugram and Delhi opens on the same point, so you can compare what the record claims against what is actually standing there. It opens on our own address until you give it something else.

Waiting for a point

A plus code off a Google Maps card, such as F38V+W3G, Sector 27, Gurugram, resolves here without a network call. A shortened maps.app.goo.gl link has to be opened once so it expands, then paste the long address from the browser bar. Nothing leaves this machine, the buttons only build addresses.

Satellite imagery shows what is built. It does not show who owns it, what is sanctioned, or where the boundary legally runs. Use it to catch the obvious contradiction, never as evidence in a report.

How much of the score the machine can reach

Forty four of two hundred scored parameters have an automated source behind them, which sounds thin until you weight it. The automated ones are the heavy ones.

The thirteen knockouts

Eleven can be checked without leaving the desk. The two that cannot are physical, which is the correct answer rather than a gap.

Nothing joins to anything

The jamabandi is keyed on khewat and khasra. The deed registry is keyed on party name and date. There is no shared identifier anywhere, so three heuristics do the work. All three are running below.

Owner name matcher

Each name is reduced to a consonant skeleton that survives transliteration, then compared token by token, with parentage weighted equally because it disambiguates far better than a given name.

Khasra lineage

Compound khasra numbers read right to left and record a history of subdivision. A deed from 1998 citing khasra 155 still describes the parcel a 2024 record calls 5073 slash 4829 slash 155.

Area reconciliation

The jamabandi gives kanal and marla, the deed gives square yards, the broker gives gaj. Records never agree exactly, so the test is a band rather than equality.

How the loop runs

You score, it watches

Open the dashboard, set every slider the way you would on a real file, and press Teach the agent this file. Your reasons go with the scores.

It solves for your weights

Non negative ridge from your parameter scores to the overall you settled on. Non negative because a parameter cannot make a property better by being worse.

It reports its own error

Leave one out, in points out of 100, and how often it lands in your verdict band. A model that cannot say how often it is wrong has no business scoring anything.

It writes in your words

Your own sentences are stored against each parameter and offered back when a new file scores similarly, so a generated card does not read like a template.

How this is meant to be worked

The inbox is the system

Every enquiry from the website arrives as an email at contact.tasalee@gmail.com with the client's own address as reply to. Answer in the thread and the client sees a normal conversation, not a ticket number.

The draft is a starting point

The assistant answers only from what is mapped, and marks anything it could not find rather than filling the gap. Read every draft before it goes. Your name is on it, not its.

Never quote a score you have not run

A first reply says what we will check and what it costs. It does not estimate a score, a price or a risk from an address alone, because that is exactly the guessing the whole business exists to replace.

The queue lives in this browser

What you log here stays on this machine. It is a working desk, not a shared CRM. Connect the backend and the same screen fetches the inbox and files replies against the property instead.
